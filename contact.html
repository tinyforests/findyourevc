Tool Information

Purpose

This tool provides address-based lookup of Ecological Vegetation Class classifications for Victoria. It is intended as a reference resource for identifying pre-1750 vegetation communities associated with specific locations.

Data Sources

EVC Data: Victorian Government Open Data Platform — Native Vegetation 2005 EVC Bioregional Conservation Status (NV2005_EVCBCS layer), accessed via Web Feature Service (WFS).

Geocoding: OpenStreetMap Nominatim service for address coordinate resolution.

Authority: EVC classifications and conservation status are maintained by the Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA).

Use Limitations

This tool provides indicative information only. EVC classifications represent modelled pre-European vegetation distributions and do not constitute site assessments or planning determinations.

For statutory planning, environmental reporting, or compliance purposes, users should refer to authoritative sources including the Victorian Biodiversity Atlas and consult with qualified ecologists or relevant authorities.

Technical Queries

For questions regarding EVC classifications, mapping methodology, or conservation status, contact the Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA) or refer to the Victorian Biodiversity Atlas.

Tool Development

This lookup tool was developed by Gardener & Son as a publicly accessible interface to state vegetation data.

Related Resources

Victorian Biodiversity Atlas: environment.vic.gov.au/biodiversity/victorian-biodiversity-atlas

DEECA Native Vegetation: environment.vic.gov.au/native-vegetation

We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands on which this data is mapped and pay our respects to Elders past, present, and emerging.

EVC classifications are derived from state-published spatial datasets and represent broad ecological patterns rather than site-specific assessments. For planning or compliance purposes, consult the relevant authority.
